Service Overview

Building Programs
That Work on the Ground.

Large-scale programs succeed or fail in the details of delivery: how communities are engaged, how institutions are strengthened and how markets respond.

We bring together field teams, technical specialists and program managers to design, roll out and manage programs in agriculture, livelihoods, enterprise development and market access. We work alongside governments, foundations, multilateral agencies and CSR partners.

Our Philosophy

Six Principles.
One Implementation Philosophy.

Every program we deliver is shaped by six principles, refined over three decades of fieldwork with communities across India.

01

Focus on the Poorest of the Poor

Programs designed first for the households most often left out.

We prioritise the most vulnerable households in targeting, design and delivery, so benefits reach those who need them most and not only those easiest to reach.

02

Participatory Approach

Communities shape the program rather than simply receive it.

Through participatory appraisal, community meetings and local institutions, people help define problems, plan solutions and monitor progress.

03

Eco-Development Approach

Livelihood gains that protect the natural resource base.

Soil, water and biodiversity are built into program design, so economic progress does not come at the cost of the environment communities depend on.

04

Equal Partnership

Communities, government and partners as genuine co-owners.

Roles, responsibilities and decisions are shared openly between communities, institutions, government and funders. That builds ownership that outlasts the program.

05

Quantum Jumps, Not Incremental Changes

Designing for step-changes in income and opportunity.

We look for interventions such as new markets, technologies and enterprise models that can transform livelihoods rather than improve them at the margins.

06

Technology-Driven Approach

Digital tools for targeting, delivery, tracking and transparency.

Mobile data collection, GIS mapping, digital MIS and traceability systems bring speed, accountability and real-time insight to programs at scale.
